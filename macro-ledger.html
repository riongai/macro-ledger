About the sugar figure. It counts total sugars — the sugar in fruit and milk as well as anything added. The 10%-of-calories cap it is measured against comes from the WHO guideline on added sugars, so fruit and milk push you toward a line they were never meant to count against. Read a day of whole fruit and yoghurt differently from the same number reached through soft drink. Whole foods use published composition data; restaurant dishes are estimated from what carries the sugar in them, because no chain publishes it.
